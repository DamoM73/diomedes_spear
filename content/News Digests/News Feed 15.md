## Dockside Pressure on Ceres as Water Prices Climb

_By Talia Mendoza, Ceres Free Press_

Ceres Station’s main docks have not been this tense in years. Cargo manifests are being rechecked, ice haulers are being inspected twice over, and Belter dockworkers report that water allotments for some outer habitats have quietly tightened. The official line from Ceres Security is that these are routine adjustments in response to market volatility. Few on the docks believe it.

Water futures have spiked sharply over the past quarter, driven by disruptions in scheduled Saturnian ice runs and increased Inner demand. Several haulers scheduled for Pallas and Vesta have been redirected to Ceres under emergency contracts. The result has been a ripple effect across the Belt, with smaller stations complaining of delayed deliveries and rising docking fees.

Star Helix contractors on Ceres deny that rationing is imminent. “There is no crisis,” a spokesperson said. “Ceres maintains strategic reserves sufficient for projected consumption.” However, independent traders confirm that short-term contracts are being offered at rates not seen since the Anderson Station incident.

Belter advocacy groups argue that any strain will be felt hardest in the lower levels, where recycled water quality already varies widely. For now, the station’s spin continues, and the tanks remain technically full. But even the perception of scarcity is enough to raise tempers in the corridors of Ceres.

## Tycho OPA Accused of Harboring Espionage Network

_By Far Olympus Enterprises, System Security Desk_

**Inners Raise Alarms**  
Sources within the UN Intelligence Directorate allege that Tycho Station has become a “staging ground” for Belt-based espionage targeting corporate and governmental assets.

**Corporate Ties Questioned**  
Vector Security, a Pope Enterprises subsidiary, is under investigation for selling surveillance tech to Tycho OPA intermediaries. Caracara Electronics denied involvement but confirmed “data breaches” at a Luna-based server hub.

**Johnson’s Denial**  
Fred Johnson dismissed the allegations, calling them “another Earther fever dream.” Tycho Free News accused Far Olympus of publishing “Inners’ propaganda” to undermine Belt independence.

**Fallout in the Belt**  
Anderson Dawes privately told Ceres leaders that “Tycho plays diplomat by day and thief by night,” suggesting growing OPA fragmentation.

## Solar Flare Distillery Faces Sabotage on Mars

_By MCRN Public News Service, Olympus Mons Feed_

**Explosion at Facility**  
A Solar Flare Distillery near Londres Nova suffered a drive fuel explosion, causing two deaths and major damage.

**Investigation Underway**  
Martian authorities suspect eco-terrorist involvement. Persephone’s Scythe denied responsibility but praised the attack as “environmental justice.”

**Corporate Rivalry**  
Red Dust Vodka executives accused competitors of staging a false flag to corner the liquor market. Masstech engineers confirmed the blast originated in a reactor valve recently replaced by contractors from Helion Materials Group.

**Market Shock**  
Martian bars reported temporary shortages of Solar Flare brands. Beltbrew sales on Ceres spiked as Belters toasted “Martian meltdown.”
