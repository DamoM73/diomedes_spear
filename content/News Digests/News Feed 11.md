## Counterfeit Ossifex Seized on Ceres
*By Tycho Free News, Health & Safety Feed*

Ceres Security confiscated 3,000 counterfeit Ossifex doses in a raid. Officials traced the shipments to smuggling routes linked to Vesta, with suspected ties to the Golden Bough Society.

Doctors from Maruttuva Kulu confirmed the seized doses contained toxic substitutes. “This isn’t just fraud, it’s murder,” said Dr. Priya Nguyen. Fourteen Belters remain hospitalized.

Investigators suspect shell companies tied to Bush Orbital Shipyards may have leaked expired compounds onto the black market, later repackaged by the Loca Greiga. Rebel Rations branding was used on some packages, raising suspicion of OPA-linked sympathizers.

Protests erupted outside Star Helix’s abandoned precinct, with Belters accusing the corporation of complicity. The Golden Bough Society denied involvement, but Dawes hinted at “traitors profiting from Belter suffering.”

## Faith of the Falling Star Terrorists Strike Mecca During Hajj in Heinous Attack
_By Lillian Sharpe, Bennett-Halabi Information Services (BHIS)_

MAKKAH AL-MUKARRAMAH— In a horrifying act of terrorism, members of the extremist cult Faith of the Falling Star launched a coordinated attack on the Hajj pilgrimage, targeting thousands of hujjaj performing their sacred duty at the Masjid al-Haram. The attack, which included suicide bombings and aerial drone strikes, has left hundreds of martyrs and wounded countless others.

The attackers, who claim to follow a new divine revelation linked to Venus, declared that traditional faiths must be destroyed to make way for their so-called “Great Awakening.” In a video statement released shortly after the attack, a masked spokesperson for the cult proclaimed:

_"The old ways are dead. You bow to false gods while the true sign has been sent to Venus. We have delivered the first warning: forsake the past or be consumed by the light of the Falling Star."_

This declaration reflects the cult’s growing extremism, which has shifted from recruitment and propaganda to outright violence against people of faith.

### Terror at the Ka’bah
The attack began just before Salat al-Maghrib, when the Haram was at peak capacity. Witnesses reported hearing takbirs followed by sudden explosions near the Maqam Ibrahim and the Marwah corridor, while armed attackers attempted to breach the Bab al-Salam entrance. Security forces engaged the assailants, preventing even greater bloodshed, but at least three bombs detonated before they could neutralize all threats.

Survivors described the horror of the attack. "One moment, we were making du’a, the next, there was fire and screaming," said a tearful hajji from Algiers. "They struck at the heart of Islam, but Allah is greater."

### A Crime Against the Ummah
Leaders from across the Muslim world have condemned the attack, calling it an unforgivable act of fasad (corruption) and fitnah (discord) against Islam.

His Eminence Grand Mufti Rashid Al-Faisal called upon all believers to stand firm:

_"They spill the blood of the innocent in the House of Allah, thinking it will shake our faith. But they have only strengthened the resolve of the Ummah. Their false revelation will be erased, and those responsible will answer to the Almighty."_

From Al-Madinah to Jakarta, from Istanbul to Lagos, millions have taken to the streets in mourning and defiance, reciting Surah Al-Fatiha for the martyrs and demanding swift justice against those who would desecrate the sanctity of the Haramayn.

### A Cult of Dajjal?
For months, intelligence agencies have warned that the Faith of the Falling Star was radicalizing, spreading its falsehood through encrypted networks, targeting the vulnerable with promises of “a new ascension.” They claim that Eros was not destruction, but the opening of a new age, with Venus as the vessel of divine will.

But their actions speak only of disbelief and oppression. Once dismissed as a fringe sect, their acts of terror now place them among the gravest threats to peace. Security officials have already begun a major crackdown, raiding suspected hideouts in Luna, Mars, and the Belt. Reports suggest that cells were planning further attacks on mosques and religious sites, believing they must “cleanse” the galaxy of the faiths they see as obsolete.

### A Unified Response
Governments across the system have declared the Faith of the Falling Star an enemy of civilization, with the United Nations, the Martian Congressional Republic, and the Outer Planets Alliance all pledging to eliminate this growing threat before they can strike again. A joint task force has already begun tracking the cult’s leaders, though many remain hidden.

As the Ummah mourns, one truth is undeniable:

_"Islam will endure, as it has endured for centuries. Their lies will fade, but the words of Allah remain eternal."_

An ice convoy owned by Pope Enterprises subsidiary Krystal Kleer was destroyed in the Pallas corridor. The eco-radical group Persephone’s Scythe claimed responsibility, calling it a “strike for water justice.”

Pope Enterprises condemned the attack as terrorism and requested that Vector Security “take all necessary measures.” The Ceres OPA refused to extradite suspected Scythe sympathizers, citing “Belt sovereignty.”

Three Belter pilots died. Maruttuva Kulu medics recovered survivors but refused to hand them to Star Helix investigators. The Voltaire Collective released a statement mocking the pacifists for “saving Pope’s lackeys.”
