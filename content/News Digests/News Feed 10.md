## Callisto Shipyards Launch 200th Munroe-Class Frigate
*By MCRN Fleet Broadcast, Callisto*

The Callisto yards celebrated the launch of their 200th Munroe-class frigate, a milestone hailed as proof of Martian industrial might.

The Munroe is optimized for escort and interdiction. Analysts at Masstech praised its integration of high-efficiency Epstein components. Dhanbad Nova provided subsystems tied to advanced propulsion.

Tycho OPA warned the expansion “sends the wrong signal.” Persephone’s Scythe called the frigates “steel proof that Mars prefers war to water.” Dawes pointed to the launch as evidence of Martian colonial arrogance.

Corentin Francisco, the notorious arms dealer, was rumoured to be offering salvaged Munroe components on the black market. Loca Greiga operatives on Callisto celebrated the launch with Beltbrew and Martian Mint liqueur, seeing opportunity in heightened demand for smuggled tech.

## Fungus Threatens Ganymede Crops
*By Bennett-Halabi Information Services (BHIS), Jovian Desk*

Scientists on Ganymede confirmed a fungal outbreak that threatens to cut crop yields by 20%. FutureVeg Habitat warned the blight could “jeopardize Belt nutrition.”

Dhanbad Nova dispatched biotech teams to test antifungal agents. Pinkwater Security increased inspections, though reports surfaced of bribes paid to overlook contaminated shipments.

Loca Greiga used front company TKO Holdings to hoard and resell unaffected produce at inflated prices. Orn Aquilo, a Ganymede crime boss, offered “protection” for dome shipments but is suspected of siphoning supplies to his own syndicate.

Maruttuva Kulu established free clinics for malnourished Belters. Persephone’s Scythe issued a manifesto condemning Inner corporations for “patent strangleholds” on resistant crops.
