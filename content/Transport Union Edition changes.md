## Chapter 1: Game Basics

- Rounding default flipped from round-up (v2.0) to round-down (TUE)
- New guidance on multiplying static numbers vs dice rolls, and on stacking bonuses/penalties (defaults to "whichever is better")
- Chapter cross-references renumbered to match TUE's restructured chapter list

## Chapter 2: Character Creation

- New origin-specific Gravity Penalties table (Belter/Earther/Martian each get distinct numeric penalties by gravity level), replacing v2.0's single generic rule

## Chapter 3: Character Traits

- Two new specialisations: Overwatch (remote tactical command) and Spin Doctor (media manipulation)

## Chapter 4: Technology & Equipment

- Weapons replaced with a full named catalogue (was five generic categories)
- Drones and mechs replaced with named, individually costed items
- Two new armour types (Light Vacuum Armor, Riot Armor), plus Faux Power Armor and a new "Gravity Specific" quality
- Entirely new Enhancement Implants (cybernetics) system

## Chapter 5: Gameplay

- Vehicle combat overhauled: new Velocity Classes, Boarding rules, Vehicular Movement, Vehicles Versus Living Targets, reworked Attacks Versus Vehicles
- Hazards damage table expanded/made more lethal at high tiers (top four categories become ranges, roughly doubled maximums)
- Rapid Reload stunt trigger/effect changed
- "(CORE)" stunt annotations dropped

## Chapter 6: Spaceships

- Travel-time tables swap Pluto for The Ring, add Kuiper Belt
- Four new ship qualities: Atmosphere Capable, Drone Bay, Altered Transponder Code, Carbon-Silicate Lace Hull
- Titanic-class example changed from "Colony generation-ship" to "Void City" (flavour only)

## Chapter 7: Future History

- Default timeline moves forward roughly 30 years, from end of Leviathan Wakes to post-Free Navy Conflict/Transport Union founding
- New NPC stat blocks: Fred Johnson, Winston Duarte, Michio Pa
- Avasarala's stat block moves to Chapter 8

## Chapter 8: Earth

- New Avasarala and Clarissa Mao stat blocks
- Head of State changes to Secretary-General Avasarala
- Capital changes NYC to Geneva; largest city NYC to Delhi
- Population drops from ~31 billion to ~29 billion
- GDP entry dropped from stat table

## Chapter 9: Mars

- Head of State changes to Prime Minister Emily Richards
- Bobbie Draper stat block substantially upgraded (Fortune, Defense, talents, new specialisation)
- New narrative material on Mars's decline and the Laconian defection

## Chapter 10: The Belt & the Outers

- v2.0's two chapters (The Belt, The Outers) merged into one; "Beyond the Ring" content split off into new Chapter 11
- New Transport Union infrastructure: Tycho-Pallas Complex, Void Cities, Transfer Stations
- New Camina Drummer stat block
- Naomi Nagata stat block substantially upgraded

## Chapter 11: Beyond the Ring

- Entirely new chapter — Medina Station, the Ring, the Slow Zone, the Survey Group, and a gazetteer of roughly twenty gate systems

## Chapter 12: Game Mastering

- Heavily reorganised, front-loaded with campaign/table guidance
- New sample campaign pitches, Session Zero process, Safety Tools/diversity/accessibility section, and table-management content
- Relationships subsystem relocated here from Chapter 14
- New Apprehension subsystem (fear/mental strain mechanic using Willpower foci)

## Chapter 13: Threats

- Hazards catalogue expanded from seven to thirteen types
- "Supporting Cast" replaced with formalised "Zeroes" tier (Field quality, Optional Potential rule, named examples)
- New adversary archetypes (Ambassador, Combat Mech, Engineer, Force Recon, Pirate Captain, Transport Union Security/Inspector, Laconian Agent, Laconian Black Ops)
- Hooligan adversary dropped
- Seven new Alien Adversaries added

## Chapter 14: Rewards

- Relationships subsystem moved out to Chapter 12
- Two honorifics renamed: "Bane of the Belt" to "Pirate Hunter", "True OPA" to "Transport Union Loyalist" (old terminology inconsistently left in surrounding flavour text)

## Chapter 15: The Expanse Series

- Two themes dropped (Arc of History, Knowledge is Power), replaced with three new ones
- Starting Points reframed around the Transport Union era and beyond
- Three series frameworks dropped (Military, Rebellion, Protomolecule), replaced with two new ones (Outlaws, Exocolonist)
- Freelancer Series reorganised; Political Series reframed around Transport Union authority
- New Beyond Canon concept ("Mutations")

## Crew Companion: Occupation Changes

- New Hacker occupation added
- Standardised structure applied across all occupations
- New equipment chapter, Enhancement Implants, and profession-specific ships added
- Journalist gains new "No Further Questions" investigative subsystem
- Pirate and Trader/Smuggler occupations substantially expanded with new narrative guidance
- Cross-book references consolidated; NPCs/employers updated for Transport Union era

## Crew Companion: New Tools of the Trades

- Entirely new chapter with no equivalent in original Trades PDFs
- Professional/profession-specific equipment, expanded computer systems and Expert Systems, wider drone catalogue, new Planetary Vehicles category, additional weapons
- New equipment qualities and flaws system
- New Making & Tinkering (crafting) and Equipment Modification systems
