A popular dice gambling game in the backrooms of Ceres' casinos, _Spin the Airlock_ is a fast-paced, high-risk game where players try to out roll—and outbluff—their opponents. Unlike its underground Belt version, the Ceres casino variant includes a structured betting system and a house edge, making it a staple of the gambling scene in the station's bustling concourses.

---
## Requirements
- 3 six-sided dice (d6) per player
- 2–6 players
- A shared pot of chips or credits

---
## Setup

1. **Buy-In:** Each player agrees on a minimum stake.
2. **Ante:** Each round starts with all players placing the ante into the pot.

---
## Gameplay
1. **Spin Ya Dice:**  Each player rolls three d6 in secret, keeping them covered.
2. **Push Ya Luck:**  Starting left of the winner from the previous round, players may:
	- **Hold da Pressure:** Stay in by matching the current bet.       
    - **Crank da Lock:** Raise the bet (others must match or fold).        
    - **Vent da Lock:** Fold and lose their ante and any prior bets.    
    Betting continues until all active players have matched the last raise.
3. **Open da Door:**   All remaining players reveal their dice. Highest hand wins the pot.

---
## Winning Hands (Highest to Lowest)
1. Triple Sixes ("Deep Black") – An instant win unless another player ties (then the pot carries over to the next round).
2. Three-of-a-Kind ("Kakka Lucky") – Any three matching numbers (higher sets beat lower).
3. Straight ("Smooth Burn") – Any three sequential numbers (1-2-3 is lowest, 4-5-6 highest).
4. Two-of-a-Kind ("Leaky Seal") – Two matching dice; the third die acts as a tiebreaker.
5. High Die (“Cold Void”) – If no players have a ranked hand, the pot is jackpotted to next round

---
## Optional Rules
- **Double Burn**: A player may re-roll one die by doubling their bet before reveal. Must declare before any other reveal.
