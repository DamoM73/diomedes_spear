## On your turn
Choose one of these:
- 1 major action + 1 minor action
- 2 minor actions

You can take them in either order. Free actions are allowed when sensible, but the GM can limit them. Talking is free, but a round is only 15 seconds.

#### Major Actions 
| Action         | Simple version                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| All-Out Attack | Hit harder (+1 damage), but you are easier to hit (-1 Defence) until your next turn  |
| Charge         | Move up to half Speed, then make a melee attack; +1 to hit if you moved 4m+ straight |
| Defend         | Focus on defence; +2 Defence until your next turn                                    |
| Melee Attack   | Attack someone within 2m using close combat                                          |
| Ranged Attack  | Shoot or throw at a target you can see                                               |
| Run            | Move up to double Speed, but you do nothing else                                     |
| Stunt Attack   | Make an attack just to generate stunt points (no damage)                             |

#### Minor Actions
| Action           | Simple version                                                          |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Activate         | Turn on or use an item or ability                                       |
| Aim              | Get +1 to your next attack                                              |
| Guard Up         | Gain +1 or +2 Defence, but take the same penalty to all rolls this turn |
| Move             | Move up to your Speed (extra actions like standing use some movement)   |
| Prepare          | Delay your action and jump in later before your next turn               |
| Press the Attack | If an enemy you hit moves away, you can follow them for free            |
| Ready            | Swap or draw items (e.g. holster gun, pull grenade)                     |
| Stand Firm       | Harder to move or knock down; enemies must win a test to do it          |

### Make an attack
When you attack:
1. Roll 3d6.
2. Add the relevant ability and focus:
3. Compare the total to the target’s Defence.
4. If you equal or beat Defence, you hit.

#### Stunt points
If any two of the 3d6 match on a successful attack roll, you generate stunt points equal to the Drama Die. You can spend them immediately on action stunts.

#### Attack Roll Modifiers
| Modifier | Circumstance                                                                                                                                           |
| :------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|    -5    | Attacker is blind or otherwise completely unable to see the target.                                                                                    |
|    -3    | Defender in heavy cover, such as a building or stone wall. Melee attacker in heavy snow.                                                               |
|    -2    | Defender in light cover, such as a hedge or the woods. Melee attacker in the mud. Ranged attack vs. defender engaged in melee combat. Combat at night. |
|    -1    | Rain, mist, or smoke obscures the defender. Combat in low light conditions.                                                                            |
|    0     | Normal circumstances.                                                                                                                                  |
|    +1    | Attacker is on higher ground. Defender is prone. Melee attacker and allies outnumber defender 2 to 1.                                                  |
|    +2    | Melee attacker and allies outnumber defender 3 to 1. Defender is drunk.                                                                                |
|    +3    | Defender is unaware of the attack.                                                                                                                     |

### Roll damage
If you hit:
1. Roll the weapon’s damage.
2. Add any modifiers the weapon or attack gives.
### Apply damage 
1. Subtract Toughness
	- Subtract Toughness (Constitution + armour) 
	- Ignore this step if the damage is _penetrating_
2. Spend Fortune
	- 1 Fortune = remove 1 damage
	- If damage reaches 0, nothing serious happens\
3. Take conditions
	- If damage remains, reduce it by taking conditions:
		- Injured → reduces damage by 1d6
		- If damage still remains (or already Injured) → take Wounded → reduces another 1d6
4. Taken out
	- If damage is still left, the target is taken out
	- The attacker decides the result (e.g. unconscious, helpless, or dying)


## Morale Tests
Characters who fail a Willpower (Morale) test attempt to break off from combat or even surrender.

Conditions triggering a morale test:
- When more than half of the side’s combatants are taken out of the fight
- When the side’s leader or champion is taken out of the fight
- When fighting on will clearly mean being trapped or surrounded
- When the other side reveals a serious or overwhelming advantage
- When they fail an opposed TN15 Persuasion test.
