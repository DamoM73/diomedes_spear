Kruger-Thompson Mining (KTM) is committed to fostering a productive and structured work environment at Mathilde Station, ensuring that all employees contribute effectively to the success of our operations. The following contract terms have been designed to maximize efficiency, reward dedication, and maintain the safety and integrity of our workforce while aligning with industry best practices.

## Key Contractual Terms

### 1. Long-Term Employment Stability
- Employees are guaranteed continuous work opportunities until the completion of designated mining operations.
- Secure, ongoing employment ensures long-term income and career stability within KTM’s operations.

### 2. Inclusive Compensation Model
- KTM provides competitive wages, with operational costs conveniently deducted to cover air, water, housing, equipment, and transportation.
- This cost-sharing model ensures employees always have access to essential services without upfront financial burden.

### 3. Performance-Based Productivity Targets
- KTM promotes a merit-based work environment, where high-performing employees are recognized and rewarded.
- Productivity expectations scale with experience, allowing motivated workers to demonstrate their dedication and advance their careers.

### 4. Personal Accountability for Workplace Safety
- KTM ensures all employees have access to the highest safety standards and training to mitigate risks.
- Employees are encouraged to take personal responsibility for adhering to safety protocols. KTM assumes no liability for negligence or failure to comply with established guidelines.

### 5. Strong Team Cohesion & Unified Workforce
- KTM values workplace harmony and maintains a strict policy against divisive activities, including unauthorized labour actions.
- Employees benefit from a structured chain of command, ensuring efficient conflict resolution through designated corporate channels.

### 6. Fair & Equitable Resource Distribution
- KTM manages air and water resources based on operational needs, ensuring that essential personnel receive priority allocations to maintain station-wide productivity.
- This adaptive rationing system prevents unnecessary waste and supports sustainable operations.

### 7. Commitment to Project Goals
- Employees are expected to demonstrate flexibility by adapting to extended shift requirements when necessary, ensuring that critical mining projects remain on schedule.
- KTM appreciates team players who understand the importance of shared success and operational continuity.

### 8. Fitness-for-Duty Policy
- Employees must maintain a standard level of physical fitness to ensure workplace safety and efficiency.
- Those unable to perform their duties for an extended period may be reassigned or provided alternative career paths, ensuring a workforce that remains healthy and capable.

### 9. Workplace Integrity & Secure Communications
- KTM enforces standard communication protocols to ensure data security and operational confidentiality.
- Employees can feel confident working in a well-monitored, transparent environment, free from external distractions or interference.

### 10. Flexible Departure Policy
- Employees who wish to transition out of Mathilde Station before their contract term concludes can utilize the company’s departure assistance program by contributing a departure fee that offsets the company’s investment in their training and resources.
- This fair exit policy ensures that workers remain committed to their roles while maintaining the option for future career flexibility.
